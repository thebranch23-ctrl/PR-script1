B2B Moving Industry Script

Prospect: Hello?
You: Hello, am I speaking with the owner of {Business Name}?
Owner: Yes, this is the owner.
You: My name is [Your Name] with P&R Acquisition Group. How are you today?
Pause for response
You: I am calling because we help local moving companies get more jobs without using Google Ads or relying only on word-of-mouth.

Are you available to take on more jobs right now, or is your schedule completely full?
Pause for response
You: I understand. We currently work with four moving companies in {Their State}. Our only focus is helping moving businesses get more booked jobs.

How do you currently get most of your moving jobs?
Pause for response
You: Thank you for sharing that. The first step we take to increase your jobs is listing your business on our affiliate platforms. We send you job requests, and you can accept, decline, or reschedule them based on your availability.

Are you currently active on your Google Business Profile?
Pause for response
You: We manage that profile for you. We post updates four times per week and automate your customer reviews.

How do you collect reviews from your clients right now?
Pause for response
You: Our system makes review collection fully automatic. Typically, ninety-five percent of clients leave a review on your Google profile.

When we combine regular posts with consistent reviews, your business ranks higher on Google. This naturally brings more direct jobs to your business.

Do you have any questions about how this works?
Answer any quick question, then move immediately to the close

Rebuttal for "How much does it cost?"

That's a great question. The exact cost depends on your moving company's size, goals, and the services that make the most sense for your business. Because every company is different, we customize our plans instead of offering a one-size-fits-all package.

For example, some moving companies prefer a performance-based model, while others choose a simple monthly retainer.

What I'd like to do is have one of our Account Managers take a deeper look at your business and put together the best option for you. They can also provide exact pricing and answer any questions you have.

What day and time would be best for them to give you a call?

Our typical entry package starts at $350/month and includes:

  • Affiliate platform setup and management, helping generate inbound moving job requests that you can accept, decline, or reschedule.
  • Google Business Profile optimization, including 3–4 posts per week to improve your visibility in local search results.
  • An automated review and referral system designed to help generate Google reviews from up to 95% of your completed customers, while also encouraging referrals.
You: I would like to show you how our Local Starter Plan works and what job volume you can expect in your area. It takes about fifteen minutes.

Would tomorrow or Thursday work better for a short call?
